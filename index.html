    How this page updates

    Claude re-reads live results pages on a schedule, checks the numbers and republishes this page. Recent updates:

      Every update is checked before it goes live. A race is skipped if its vote shares add up to more than 100%, if the share counted goes backwards, or if a projected race would change hands; seat counts are never allowed to fall. If too much of an update fails these checks, none of it is published.

      About these numbers. Results are read from ABC News live results pages, which use Associated Press vote data, and updated by Claude every few minutes on election night. They can lag the source by a few minutes, and outlets call races at different speeds. Early counts can shift a lot as mail ballots and late-reporting counties come in. The House control projection is from Decision Desk HQ, made at 9:09 PM ET.

      Senate tallies include the 65 senators not on this year's ballot. Democrats need 51 seats for Senate control because Vice President JD Vance breaks a 50–50 tie. House control takes 218 of 435 seats.
